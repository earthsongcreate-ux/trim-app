* **Apple App Store Processing:** All payments are charged to your Apple ID account at the confirmation of purchase.
* **Auto-Renewal:** Your subscription will automatically renew at the current price and duration unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period.

You have complete control over your subscription:

* **Effect of Cancellation:** If you cancel your subscription, you will retain access to Trim IQ premium features until the end of your current billing cycle. After that, your account will revert to the free tier.
* **No Refunds:** Payments are non-refundable, and we do not provide refunds or credits for any partial-month subscription periods.
