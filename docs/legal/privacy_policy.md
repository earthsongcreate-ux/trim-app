# Trim Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [Date]

Trim ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience when using the Trim mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information, particularly regarding your financial data.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
When you create an account, we may collect personal information such as your name, email address, and authentication credentials (e.g., Apple ID or Google ID).

### B. Financial Data
To provide our core services—identifying unused subscriptions and negotiating bills—we require access to your transaction history. 
* **Third-Party Integration:** We use **Plaid Inc. ("Plaid")** to securely connect to your bank accounts. We never see, collect, or store your banking credentials (usernames or passwords). 
* **Read-Only Access:** We only request read-only access to your transaction data. We do not have the ability to move money or alter your bank accounts.
* **Data Stored:** We securely store normalized transaction data (amount, date, merchant) necessary to provide our intelligence and savings features.

### C. Device and Usage Data
We collect standard device information (e.g., OS version, device model) and usage telemetry to improve the App's performance and stability.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:
* Provide, maintain, and improve the Trim service.
* Analyze your transactions to identify savings opportunities, duplicate charges, and active subscriptions.
* Facilitate subscription cancellations on your behalf, only when explicitly authorized by you.
* Process payments for your Trim Premium subscription.
* Communicate with you regarding security updates, account alerts, and customer support.

## 3. Data Security and Storage

We implement bank-level security measures to protect your data:
* **Encryption:** All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
* **Local Security:** Session tokens are stored securely in the iOS Keychain. Access to the App can be protected via Biometric Authentication (Face ID / Touch ID).
* **No Selling of Data:** **We do not and will never sell your personal or financial data to third parties.**

## 4. Third-Party Disclosures

We share data with third parties only when necessary to provide our services:
* **Plaid:** For linking financial accounts. By using Trim, you also agree to Plaid’s Privacy Policy.
* **Cloud Infrastructure Providers:** (e.g., AWS, GCP) for secure hosting and database management.
* **Apple:** For managing in-app purchases and subscriptions.

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:
* **Disconnect Accounts:** You can revoke Trim's access to your bank accounts at any time within the App.
* **Delete Your Data:** You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated financial data by contacting support or using the in-app deletion tool.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at privacy@trim.example.com.
